Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Which sites are supported by StreamFab?
StreamFab supports over 1,000 platforms: YouTube (videos, Shorts, playlists), TikTok (watermark-free), Twitter/X, Instagram (Reels & posts), Facebook, Twitch (clips and VODs), SoundCloud, Vimeo, Dailymotion, and many more.
Why is StreamFab so much faster than NoTube?
NoTube relies on centralized, overloaded servers with artificial queues designed to display ads. StreamFab leverages an optimized yt-dlp core with 8-thread multi-fragment downloading and direct FFmpeg hardware-accelerated processing.
Can I download only a portion of a video?
Yes! That is one of StreamFab's signature features. Expand the Built-in Audio/Video Trimmer section, enter the start and end timestamps (e.g., 01:30 to 02:45), and only that segment will be extracted and converted.
How do I download an entire playlist?
Simply paste the playlist link. StreamFab automatically parses all videos, lets you check or uncheck the ones you want, and downloads them in your preferred format.
