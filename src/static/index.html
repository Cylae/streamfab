Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Which sites are supported by StreamFab?
StreamFab uses yt-dlp extractors. Site availability varies and external network downloaders are disabled in web mode. Examples include: YouTube (videos, Shorts, playlists), TikTok (watermark-free), Twitter/X, Instagram (Reels & posts), Facebook, Twitch (clips and VODs), SoundCloud, Vimeo, Dailymotion, and many more.
How does processing work?
The server resolves media URLs using yt-dlp. MP3 and WAV can be encoded in the browser. Other formats, subtitles, playlists, or unsupported browser codecs use server-side FFmpeg.
Can I download only a portion of a video?
Yes! That is one of StreamFab's signature features. Expand the Built-in Audio/Video Trimmer section, enter the start and end timestamps (e.g., 01:30 to 02:45), and only that segment will be extracted and converted.
How do I download an entire playlist?
Simply paste the playlist link. StreamFab automatically parses all videos, lets you check or uncheck the ones you want, and downloads them in your preferred format.
